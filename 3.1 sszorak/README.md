# Sszorak

- Look for winds outside the arena
  - Place green orbs at the opposite end of the arena to the wind
- Circles + lines
  - Pair up so the line goes through your partner
  - (MYTH) Lines can also match at a 90° angle
  - (MYTH) Most players self-mitigate, ping if you don't
- Soak the green frontal cone

## 🏆 Mythic Strategy

### Fixate 🎯
- The farthest player is fixated - assign who stands farthest
  - Shortly after the encounter starts
  - Shortly after each trigger

### Trigger ⏳
- Raid groups up before the enrage (at least 14 players)
- The assigned player triggers it by running in (NSRT timer)
- When triggered, everyone gets star-shaped lines
  - The damage ends roughly where the indicator ends (short)
  - Assign spread positions
