# Entombed Sentinels

- Stay 40 yards apart
- Bosses swap after the intermission (boss positions, players stay)
- (MYTH) 8 players get an orange circle
  - Do not touch players without a circle
  - Move to players with a circle

## 🟢 Green
- Adds 👹
  - Bait near the boss, not near the blood
  - Cleave the first add
  - Focus kill the second add before the intermission
- Run over blobs
  - Immunities for the soaks near the intermission
  - (HC) Destroyed blobs spawn spikes towards the boss

## 🔴 Red
- Dispels 🌀
- Group soak on the edge
  - Minimum 8 players
  - Spread along the edge to reduce the used space
  - (HC) Every soaker spawns a void that grows

## ⏸️ Intermission
- Poison values are only shown shortly
  - Your own is visible on the debuff display
- Players with 1 poison use `/ping [@player]`
- Players with 2 poison move to the boss
