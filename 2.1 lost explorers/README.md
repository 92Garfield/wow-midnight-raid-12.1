# Lost Explorers

- 3 turtles 🐢 - kill at the same time
- No more than 2 of them stacked

## Positioning

```
Start:          ⚓ Nama to 📦 Gebbo | 📜 Iku behind 📦 Gebbo
Second shells:  ⚓ Nama to 📜 Iku   | behind 📦 Gebbo
2nd fish:       ⚓ Nama to 📦 Gebbo | 📜 Iku to the center, focus 📦 Gebbo
Gebbo at 10%:   ⚓ Nama to 📜 Iku
```

## 📦 Trader Gebbo
- Not tanked, wanders around
- Throws stuff:
  - Box / fish: picked up by a player, used on a turtle - one per turtle, turtle is empowered
    - (MYTH) The box applies a DoT to the entire raid
  - Mushroom + bomb: jump on the mushroom to bounce over the explosion
    - 📜 Iku + ⚓ Nama to the center to bait the mushroom
    - Drop the bomb ahead of Gebbo's movement
    - (MYTH) Only 1 mushroom

## ⚓ First Mate Nama
- Tanked
- 3 group soaks at a time
  - Closest target on every leap
  - Soak with the entire raid
  - Soak near the edge (outside of the inner circle)
  - (MYTH) Permanent void zones
- Shells are baited by melee
  - Bait them to the outside of the arena

## 📜 Scrollsage Iku
- Blink nova
  - Edge of the arena
  - Behind 📦 Gebbo, slightly in front of 📜 Iku
- 🔴 Red & 🔵 blue circles on players
  - Pair up (🔴 + 🔵), overlapping the circles
  - After the voids are down, run into the opposite void to clear
  - Fire targets ranged, frost targets melee
