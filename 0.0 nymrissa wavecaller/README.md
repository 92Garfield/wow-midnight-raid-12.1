# Nymrissa Wavecaller

## Chilling Frost ❄️
- Debuff on players
- Places an orb on the player's position on every tick (1.5s)
- (HC) Unsoaked orbs:
  - Wipe the raid after some time
- Soaked orb:
  - (MYTH) Raid damage 
  - Creates a frost zone
    - (MYTH) Frost zones are removed by the tank frontal
  - The soaking player gets a DoT and +20% damage taken from frost orbs - stackable

## Adds 👹
- Bubbles on the side of the room show the spawns
- Boss is tanked on the adds
- Add reaching the center orb → wipe
- (MYTH) focus Frostscale Murloc - makes other nearby murlocs immune

## Whirlpool 🌊 - after the add phase
- Ring around the room
- Find the safespot (gap)

## 🏆 Mythic Strategy
- Boss is tanked on the nearest 2 add groups only
- Assign add killers: 2 groups (left / right) of 2-5 players each (ranged)
- Assign immunities to clear orbs during the Whirlpools: 1:47, 3:37, 5:27

### Orbs ❄️
- When placing an orb, soak 1 if the raid is healthy and you have a defensive
- Place orbs close to existing puddles
- Place orbs near the center
  - ✅ Ranged can reach the far adds better from the center
  - ✅ Adds won't reach the puddles
  - ❌ The tank frontal through the center can hit players on the other side
- Stray orbs are soaked by the 3rd tank
- Orbs near the boss are soaked by the offtank
