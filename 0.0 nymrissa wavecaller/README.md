# Nymrissa Wavecaller

## Chilling Frost ❄️
- Debuff on players
- Places an orb on the player's position on every tick (1.5s)
- (HC) Unsoaked orbs:
  - Wipe the raid after some time
- Soaked orb:
  - (MYTH) Raid damage 
  - Creates a frost zone
    - (MYTH) Frost zones are removed by the tank frontal
  - The soaking player gets a DoT and +20% damage taken from frost orbs - stackable

## Adds 👹
- Bubbles on the side of the room show the spawns
- Boss is tanked on the adds
- Add reaching the center orb → wipe
- (MYTH) focus Frostscale Murloc - makes other nearby murlocs immune

## Whirlpool 🌊 - after the add phase
- Ring around the room
- Find the safespot (gap)
